Every night, on cue, she smiles into the camera, sells the odds, and promises you can always take a break. Tonight, someone holds her to it.

“Back yourself,” Robyn signs off, every night at nine, smiling into the ring light like she’s just scored. “And remember, you can always take a break.”

Twelve seconds from her bed in halls; twelve hundred quid a month from a betting app. The contract wants the rest of her: porridge, physio, matches, the view from her kitchen window. Authentic. Daily.

Six weeks ago tonight, a gambler sent her a screenshot: ‘TAKE A BREAK ACTIVATED (6 WEEKS)’. Her ad found him anyway. Every morning since, he’s sent last night’s clip, paused mid-smile.
