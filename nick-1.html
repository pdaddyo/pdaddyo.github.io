Welcome

La Luna Vida is a mobile massage and reiki service covering Prestwich and the surrounding areas. Receive a massage or reiki session in the comfort of your own home and leave the travelling to us.

We look forward to seeing you :)

Before Booking

Please review the following details to ensure a smooth and relaxing session in your home.

Space Requirements

The treatment bed measures 70 × 30 inches. Please ensure you have sufficient space for the bed and for the therapist to move safely around it.

Location & Travel

We are based in Prestwich, North Manchester. A small travel charge may apply depending on location surrounding areas.

Timing & Consultation

Appointment times include an additional 20 minutes for consultation and setup/pack-down (e.g. a 60-minute treatment requires 80 minutes total). All treatments include a short tailored consultation.

Group Bookings

Planning a group session? Please allow up to 15 minutes between sessions for changeover.

Important Policy Note: The treatment bed must be used for all sessions. If the treatment space is unsuitable upon arrival and causes cancellation, the full treatment fee will be charged. If you would like to discuss alternative treatment locations, please contact us before booking.
